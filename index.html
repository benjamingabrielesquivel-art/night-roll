House rules

  • One camera, one night, 27 frames. No retakes and no peeking.
  • We start at the table over drinks and end on the walk home.
  • Every frame is a feeling, not a shot list. We agree on what fits, shoot it, then we both answer the question.
  • Flash on for anything within three steps: we switch it on and wait for its little ready light. For lit signs and windows further away, flash off and hold very still.
  • We wind the wheel until it stops after every shot.
  • We can skip anything, or deal a spare. We jot one line per frame so we can match the prints later.
